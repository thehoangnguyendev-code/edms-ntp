## R3 Training sources
Four Report Types (category Training), each a code-defined provider: **Training Records**, **Training Activities**, **Training Participants**, **Training Compliance**.
- Permission: a person needs the permission of the screen the type reports on (`training.record.view`; `training.activity.view` or `training.compliance.view`; `training.compliance.view`). A template author cannot give a person a source the person may not see.
- The Training module's own rule applies on top of the template scope: without `training.scope.all` a person sees the own department only, whatever scope the template gives (for participants: the activities of the own department and the people of the own department). A person with no department sees nothing (not an error). The business-unit scope is not offered for Training.
- Training Compliance uses `TrainingComplianceService.coverageSql(...)`, the one definition the Compliance screens use, for every department: a report cannot differ from the screen. `row_id` was added to that query so one row of the report is one person and one requirement.
- Never a column: the content hash, reasons of absence or of adding a person, remarks, exemptions. The void reason of a record is a column on purpose (the Training Records screen shows it to the same people).

## R4 Security, audit and storage sources
Six Report Types: **Authentication Events**, **Electronic Signatures** (category Audit; `audit.view` and `audit.export`; whole-system scope; a run names a date range of at most 92 days, with the Audit Trail messages), **Users and Access Profiles** (`settings.user.view`; all four scopes), **Access Review Items** and **Knowledge Share Links** (`security.access_review.view`; whole-system scope), **Storage Usage** (`settings.storage_location.view`; whole-system scope).
- Never a column: user agent, device, details of an event, token or its hash, verification id, checksums, email, phone, date of birth, address, identity number, password data, free-text decision notes, bucket or file names.
- **Backup & Restore** source: built as the audit records of the module only (see "Follow-up: Backup and Restore Activity"); the backup sets and restore requests themselves are not reported.
